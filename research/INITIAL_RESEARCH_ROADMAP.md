This is a research roadmap. The existing supervisor executes the currently requested parameter checks and initialization workflow; listing future protein/production/PMF stages does not establish that their resources, parameters or simulations are already available or validated.

| Stage | Work | Evidence needed before proceeding |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Finish and audit force-field assignment | Complete COP10/GRAFT AM1-BCC, retain completed PC4, review GAFF2 bonded terms and analog penalties. Check net charges, atom ordering, masses, parameter completeness and reacted-site chemistry. | Converged charge calculations and audited complete topologies; no placeholder constants. |
| 2. Minimize the solvated prototype | Reuse hash-linked accepted patch/water coordinates; assemble compatible Amber/GAFF2/TIP3P topology; resolve steric contacts. | GROMACS preprocessing passes and minimization meets the configured force threshold with finite energies. |
| 3. Check dynamics and initial equilibration | First run the short initialization test, then extend NVT progressively. Select pressure/area control appropriate to a solid-supported interface rather than blindly scaling the slab. | No constraint failures or energy instability; temperature, pressure and structure monitored. A0.2ps check is only initialization, not equilibrium. |
| 4. Build a representative coating/substrate and validate it alone | Assess PC chain length/end effects, bulk reference density, film thickness/loading, polymer DP, surface attachments and interchain crosslinks. Establish an explicit model matrix for unresolved experimental quantities. Compare hydration profiles, coating height/coverage and ion partitioning. | Reproducible stable coating models; uncertainties in PC grade, composition basis, graft/crosslink density and experimental thickness remain explicit. Do not equate the current0.6g/cm³ starting patch with equilibrated bulk PC. |
| 5. Prepare protein structures | Select fibrinogen biological assembly; evaluate missing/disordered regions, glycans, disulfides, protonation and ligands. Evaluate full-length FXII or a clearly defined domain model;8OS5 is N-terminal domains only. Use compatible Amber-family protein/glycan/ion conventions. | Defined modeled sequences/assemblies with documented reconstructions and limitations; no silent fragment substitution. |
| 6. Validate protein-only controls | Solvate/equilibrate each protein separately under the same proposed temperature/salt/protonation conditions as interface simulations. Confirm those conditions from experiment or label them provisional. | Stable protein structure/secondary structure and appropriate handling of flexible regions; numerical stability alone does not validate all conformations. |
| 7. Build protein–surface starting ensembles | Prepare fibrinogen and FXII as separate systems with multiple initial orientations/distances. Include bare-PC and coated-PC controls with matched conditions. | No initial artificial clashes/penetration; enough water/box clearance and sensible surface coverage. Estimate total atoms/memory and GPU/HPC needs before costly runs. |
| 8. Run replicated unbiased MD | Start with pilot trajectories, then select production lengths from relaxation/sampling evidence and available compute. Use independent velocities/initial orientations, not one trajectory. | Repeated observations and sampling checks; inability to observe an adsorption event is not by itself evidence of antifouling. |
| 9. Calculate adsorption free energies if needed | For a defined protein/orientation and representative coating states, construct umbrella-sampling or other suitable free-energy calculations. Check restraints, window overlap, hysteresis and uncertainty; include surface relaxation and finite-size effects. | A converged, appropriately defined PMF/free-energy estimate, not instantaneous interaction energy. If MD characterization alone answers the question, this is an optional branch. |
| 10. Integrate mechanism and uncertainty | Compare bare/coated PC and both proteins using contact probabilities/residues, distance distributions, hydrogen bonds, hydration changes, coating rearrangement and protein structural response. Combine independent-replicate uncertainty and any validated PMFs. | Reproducible reports separating numerical/model validation, statistical evidence and experimental interpretation. Adsorption/conformational MD does not establish proteolytic FXII activation. |

Resource decisions: the current cloud instance is CPU-only. Large fibrinogen/full-FXII interface systems and replicated production/free-energy calculations require atom-count and compute-budget estimates before selecting trajectory lengths or promising completion dates. Salt/pH/temperature are not yet established for the target; fixed protonation is not explicit constant-pH dynamics.
